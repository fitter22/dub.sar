# The Tablet Paradigm

In modern programming, code is often organized into modules, functions, and objects. In Mesopotamian mathematics, computation was embodied in physical clay tablets (`IM.GID.DA` or *round tablets* `IM.GUG`).

---

## Scribal Method

A Mesopotamian mathematical tablet was an immutable artifact containing:

1. A statement of what is given (the problem).
2. The procedural calculation steps (the recipe).
3. The verified conclusion (the result).

DUB.SAR adopts this structure directly. Execution models the scribe performing calculations on temporary moist clay, producing an inscribed, permanent mathematical record.
