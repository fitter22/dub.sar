# Examples Catalog

DUB.SAR includes a variety of executable sample tablets illustrating historical calculations and modern algorithms.

---

## Historical Mesopotamian Archaeology

- **Babylonian Square Root of 2** (`examples/babylonian_sqrt2.dub`, `examples/babylonian_sqrt2_scholar.dub`):
  Computes $\sqrt{2}$ using the iterative Heron-Babylonian reciprocal method from tablet YBC 7289 ($1;24,51,10$).
- **Pythagorean Triples & Plimpton 322** (`examples/geometry_triangle.dub`, `examples/geometry_triangle_scholar.dub`):
  Constructs exact right triangles, verifies Pythagorean consistency, and determines inclination and feed ratios.
- **Ea-Nasir Copper Trade Account** (`examples/ea_nasir.dub`, `examples/ea_nasir_scholar.dub`):
  Tabulates merchant copper shipments, calculates cumulative weights in minas and talents, and verifies silver payments.

---

## Numerical Algorithms

- **Calendar Intercalation Cycle Search** (`examples/intercalation.dub`, `examples/intercalation_scholar.dub`):
  Iterates over candidate lunar and solar year cycles, discovering the optimal 128-year leap cycle.
- **Discrete & Fast Fourier Transforms** (`examples/fourier_dft.dub`, `examples/fourier_dft_scholar.dub`):
  Computes forward and inverse Fourier spectrum decompositions using directed quantity vector rotations.
- **Language Conformance Suite** (`examples/conformance.dub`, `examples/conformance_scholar.dub`):
  Validates arithmetic, unit metrology, procedures, and determination records.
