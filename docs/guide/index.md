# Language Guide

The DUB.SAR Language Guide provides in-depth documentation of all language syntax, semantics, and execution mechanics.

---

## Guide Topics

- **[Tablet Structure](tablet-structure.md)**: Scribal sections (`problem`, `recipe`, `result`) and tablet composition.
- **[Source Modes](source-modes.md)**: Tablet Mode (cuneiform), Scholar Mode (Latin keywords), and mixed mode interoperability.
- **[Quantities & Expressions](quantities-and-expressions.md)**: Identifiers, numeric literals, operators, and precedences.
- **[Calculations](calculations.md)**: Infix expressions, postfix pipelines, and built-in mathematical functions.
- **[Determinations](determinations.md)**: Record structures, field declarations, and member access.
- **[Domains & Selection](domains-and-selection.md)**: Bounded search loops, conditions, and the `retain` statement.
- **[Sequences & Tables](sequences-and-tables.md)**: Working tablets, array operations, length, and indexing.
- **[Units & Metrology](units.md)**: Standard unit table, dimensional algebra, and historical systems.
- **[Numbers & Sexagesimal](numbers.md)**: Sexagesimal notation, place values, fractions, and regular reciprocal calculations.
- **[Tablet Archive](archive.md)**: Persistent tablet schemas, content hashing, versioning, and consultation.
