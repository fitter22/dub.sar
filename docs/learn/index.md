# Learn DUB.SAR

Welcome to the DUB.SAR tutorial series. This learning path introduces scribal computation step by step, progressing from simple expressions to complex bounded mathematical searches and persistent archives.

---

1. **[First Tablet](first-tablet.md)**: Tablet structure, problem statements, and results.
2. **[Quantities & Expressions](quantities.md)**: Numbers, variables, and mathematical expressions.
3. **[Exact Sexagesimal Arithmetic](exact-arithmetic.md)**: Working with base-60 places and arbitrary precision.
4. **[Units & Dimensional Safety](units.md)**: Metrology, unit checking, and dimensionally safe operations.
5. **[Postfix Calculation Pipelines](pipelines.md)**: Stack-oriented evaluation and mathematical verbs.
6. **[Bounded Mathematical Search](search.md)**: Domains, iteration, and convergence loops.
7. **[Determinations & Selection](selection.md)**: Structured candidate records and optimal value retention.
8. **[Sequences & Structured Data](sequences.md)**: Working with lists, tabular sequences, and lookups.
9. **[Tablet Archive](archive.md)**: Consulting persistent records, working tablets, and inscriptions.
10. **[Complete Example](complete-example.md)**: A complete astronomical leap-cycle search algorithm.
